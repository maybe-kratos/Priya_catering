Booking Info

Simple & Transparent Booking

We keep it straightforward — no hidden charges, no surprises. Just great food and clear terms.

Booking Amount — 10%
A non-refundable 10% booking amount confirms your date. This secures our team and resources exclusively for your event.
Advance — 40% More
An additional 40% advance is collected before the event, bringing total pre-payment to 50%. This covers ingredient sourcing and preparation.
Balance — 50% After
The remaining 50% is due after successful completion of your event. Pay when you're happy — that's our promise.
Rescheduling Policy
Life happens! You can reschedule once at no extra cost, as long as the new date is within 2 months of the original booking.
